Deep-Dive Case Study

EstateWise Realty: ₹2,787 Cr Real Estate Sales & BI Intelligence

How structured SQL schema architecture, DAX KPIs, and Power BI dashboards provided multi-city clarity across 2,600+ transactions.

1. Business Problem & Objectives

EstateWise Realty operated across 9 major cities (Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Nashik, Chennai, Nagpur) with over 140 luxury and residential properties. Sales managers lacked consolidated visibility into sales velocity, channel attribution, downpayment health, and regional price appreciation. Transaction logs were siloed, leading to delayed executive decision-making.

2. Relational Database Schema Design

Engineered two normalized MySQL tables: properties (Property Master containing project name, builder, city, locality, property type, price/sqft, base price, possession status, amenities score) and property_bookings (Sales Transactions containing booking dates, buyer details, sales channels, discounts, down payments, and loans) linked via Foreign Key constraint with strategic indexes on booking_date, agent_name, and city.

3. Key Insights & Discoveries

  • Sales Velocity: Apartments commanded 57.54% of transaction volume, followed by Villas (14.63%) and Independent Houses (12.63%).
  • Regional Powerhouses: Ahmedabad (₹3.85Bn) and Bangalore (₹3.46Bn) led in total revenue generated, while Pune recorded the highest average property base price (~₹17M).
  • Financing Breakdown: Home loans funded 47.75% of purchases, with Full Payments accounting for 36.68% and Builder Finance at 15.57%.
  • YoY Expansion: Utilizing SQL window functions (LAG()), computed annual city-level revenue delta and YoY growth percentages to identify emerging high-yield micro-markets.

4. Tech Stack & Methodologies

Retail & Supply Chain

UrbanMart: Retail Sales & Inventory Optimization Dashboard

Tracking customer lifetime value (CLV) and inventory health across 6,000+ SKU transactions using MySQL and Power BI.

1. The Challenge

UrbanMart managed 6,240 active SKUs across regional distribution centers. High inventory holding costs and delayed visibility into slow-moving categories were squeezing operating margins. Management needed real-time visibility into SKU velocity, stock turnover ratio, and customer repeat purchase cadence.

2. Analytical Approach & SQL

Designed a 2-table schema connecting customers, orders, and inventory. Applied SQL window functions (RANK(), ROW_NUMBER(), and LAG()) to calculate inter-purchase times and RFM tiers. Built an inventory aging matrix categorizing SKUs into healthy (>10 weeks turnover), moderate, and high-risk (>18 weeks holding).

3. Quantifiable Results

  • Enabled inventory managers to pinpoint $450K+ in stagnant stock for targeted promotional liquidation.
  • Improved overall stock turnover ratio to 4.2x across core retail categories (Electronics, Apparel, Home Goods).
  • Direct connection between Power BI dashboard and MySQL reduced weekly report generation from 6 hours to zero.
